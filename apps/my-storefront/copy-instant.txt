The important architectural decision is:

Keep Medusa's Product as the commerce/catalog container, but make the storefront presentation variant-aware.

A variant is the actual sellable SKU, while the product remains the grouping/container for options, images, apparel information, etc.
lib/data/products.ts → storefront product retrieval
lib/data/variants.ts → individual variant retrieval
lib/data/vendor/products.ts → vendor CRUD
lib/util/vendor/* → vendor payload construction
ExtendedMarketplaceProduct → storefront projection
packages/shared/* → shared domain rules
default Medusa product UI → existing storefront behavior
